Each new cube snaps to the selected cube. Use the length, width, and height sliders to make any side as thin as 0.05 m (5 cm), in 0.01 m (1 cm) steps. You can also drag a face handle to resize that axis.
Mirrors all frame cubes left-to-right across the vehicle centerline.
Vertical tilt: 0°
The starter is one small, solid 0.45 m block on the vehicle. Click and drag a block’s solid body to move it across the plane; drag the red/green/blue face dots to stretch one axis, yellow corner dots to expand all dimensions, and the pink ring to rotate it horizontally. Use ROTATE VERTICALLY to tilt it up/down. Keyboard: ←/→ moves on X; ↑/↓ moves vertically on Y; hold Shift + ↑/↓ to move sideways on Z; Page Up/Down also changes height. All manipulators stay attached to the selected block. Scroll to zoom; drag empty space to orbit; right-drag to pan. Expanding the block costs credits according to its volume. Extra solid blocks can be bought and connected.
